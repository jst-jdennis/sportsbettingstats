Casino Hotels:
Casino Win for the nine casino hotels was $294.9 million for August 2026, reflecting a decrease of 5.5%
when compared to $311.9 million reported for August 2025. Year-to-date Casino Win was $2.01 billion
through August 2026, reflecting an increase of 1.8% compared to the prior year-to-date period. The Monthly
Gross Revenue Reports are posted online at https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-
gaming-enforcement-home/financial-and-statistical-information/monthly-gross-revenue-reports/
Internet Gaming:
Internet Gaming Win for the casinos and their partners was $259.3 million for August 2026, reflecting growth
of 4.4% when compared to $248.4 million reported for August 2025. Year-to-date Internet Gaming Win was
$2.13 billion through August 2026, reflecting growth of 13.2% when compared to $1.88 billion for the prior
year-to-date period. The Monthly Internet Gaming Gross Revenue Reports are posted online at
https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/financial-and-
statistical-information/monthly-internet-gross-revenue-reports/
Sports Wagering:
Sports Wagering Gross Revenue for the casinos, racetracks, and their partners was $61.4 million for August
2026, reflecting a decrease of 25.0% when compared to $81.9 million reported for August 2025. The decline
for the month primarily reflects a later start to the fall sports calendar for certain sports compared to August
2025, including college football, as well as a lower overall win percentage. Year-to-date Sports Wagering
Gross Revenue was $671.4 million through August 2026, reflecting a decrease of 5.3% when compared to
$708.7 million reported for the prior year-to-date period. The Monthly Sports Wagering Tax Returns are
posted online at https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-
home/financial-and-statistical-information/monthly-sports-wagering-revenue-reports/
Total Gaming Revenue and Gross Revenue Taxes:
Total Gaming Revenue for casinos, racetracks, and their partners was $615.7 million for August 2026,
reflecting a decrease of 4.1% when compared to $642.2 million reported for August 2025. Year-to-date
Total Gaming Revenue was $4.81 billion through August 2026, reflecting 5.4% growth when compared to
$4.57 billion reported for the prior year-to-date period. Total Gross Revenue Taxes were $81.5 million for
August 2026 and $674.2 million for year-to-date through August 2026.
