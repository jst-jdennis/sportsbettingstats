RE: Industry Guidance 2024-06
Advertisement Requirements – Gambling Assistance Messaging
Dear Certificate Holders and Operators:
The Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board (“Board”) is issuing clarifying guidance pertaining to advertising
requirements for all licensees of the Board. The Board’s regulations relating to advertising (see 58 Pa. Code
§§ 501a.7 and 813a.2 require all advertisements of PGCB licensed and regulated gaming operators to contain the
required gambling assistance message. The Board has been made aware of instances of paid sponsorships,
billboards, and advertisements that do not include the gambling assistance message. Any use of an operator’s brand
name must include the required gambling assistance message.
The Board intends this clarifying guidance to apply to all signage in or at sports venues and events,
billboards, television advertisements, sponsorship branding, etc. If you have questions on whether your
publicly displayed branding is required to display the gambling assistance message, please provide a rendering
of the branding for our review and approval.
Please provide confirmation that all advertisements adhere to the Board’s clarified advertising
requirement by January 1, 2025. If you have any questions, please contact Elizabeth Lanza, Director of
Compulsive and Problem Gambling at 717-346-8322 or elanza@pa.gov.
Sincerely,
