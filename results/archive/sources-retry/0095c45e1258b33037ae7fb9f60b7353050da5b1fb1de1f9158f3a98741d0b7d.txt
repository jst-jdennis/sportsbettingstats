Memo
To: Colorado Limited Gaming Control Commission
From: Tseko Ivanov, Gaming Controller
Cc: Chris Schroder
Date: July 16, 2026
Re: May 2026 Gaming Fund Financial Statement Presentation
Following are highlights from the Gaming Fund financial statements ending May 31, 2026.
Statement of Revenues, Expenditures, and Changes in Fund Balance
Gaming tax revenues have increased by $8,427,611 or 5.3% over May of last year. Total
Revenues for the Limited Gaming Fund as of May 31, 2026 were $168,584,428, a 3.7% increase
over May 2025.
Total expenditures for the period ending May 31, 2026 were approximately $19.5 million. This
represents a 10.3% increase over this time last year.
The largest increase is in the Salaries and Benefits, which increased by $1,332,499 or 14.1%
over last year. This is due to a 5.7% increase in the average cost of health, dental and life
expenditures this fiscal year, 11 more employees in May of this year compared to last year, and
the PERA Direct Distribution expenditure increase of $10,353 over last year. Capital outlay
expenditures increased $455,140 or 100% over last year. That is due to the move of Gaming’s
Central City/Black Hawk office and a new camera system installed in Cripple Creek.
The excess of total revenues over expenditures for May was $149,098,195. This is a 2.9%
increase over last year and represents the amount we could distribute as of May 31, 2026.
Statement of Budget to Actual
Total revenues collected through May 31, 2026 were 93.8% of budgeted. Total expenditures were
80.9% of budgeted, which is below the 91.7% of the fiscal year that has elapsed. In addition, the
excess of revenues over expenditures for May was 95.7% of budgeted.
Please feel free to contact me if you have any questions on the Gaming Fund financial statements.

BASIS AND PURPOSE FOR RULE 7
The purpose of Rule 7 is to establish a Sports Betting Operations Fee to defray the cost
of regulating the sports betting industry, specify the requirements regarding the
certification, assessment and security of sports betting systems and kiosks, direct
Sports Betting Operations to establish internal control procedures, including accounting
controls, outline reporting requirements and the computation of taxes, establish
geofence and sports betting account requirements, and to outline procedures for
change control. The statutory basis for Rule 7 is found in sections 44-30-102, C.R.S.,
44-30-201, C.R.S., 44-30-202, C.R.S., 44-30-203, C.R.S., 44-30-302, C.R.S., 44-30-

RESOLUTION CONCERNING ADOPTION OF EMERGENCY
AMENDMENTS TO DIVISION OF GAMING, SPORTS BETTING RULES 7.6,
7.7, and 7.11, 1 C.C.R. 207-2
WHEREAS, on April 13, 2026, the Colorado General Assembly passed
Senate Bill (SB) 26-131, Sports Betting Protections and, in connection therewith,
making an appropriation;
WHEREAS, on June 2, 2026, Governor Jared S. Polis signed SB 26-131;
WHEREAS, SB 26-131 takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on August 12, 2026;
WHEREAS, section 4 of SB 26-131 in part prohibits internet sports betting
operators from accepting deposits using a credit card, either directly or indirectly,
including through an account funded by a credit card, in connection with the
acceptance of a sports bet;
WHEREAS, a temporary or emergency rule may be adopted under section
24-4-103(6)(a), C.R.S., if an agency finds that immediate adoption of a rule is
imperatively necessary and that compliance with the requirements of section 24-
4-103, C.R.S., would be contrary to the public interest;
WHEREAS, the immediate adoption of the emergency amendments to Rules
7.6 Sport betting system requirements, 7.7 Sports betting kiosks, and 7.11 Sports
betting accounts, 1 C.C.R. 207-2, which amend the rules that currently explicitly
permit credit cards to prohibit deposits using a credit card, either directly or
indirectly, in connection with the acceptance of a sports bet, are imperatively
necessary to comply with newly enacted law so the rules conform to the provisions
of SB 26-131, and compliance with the procedural requirements of section 24-4-103,
C.R.S., would be contrary to public interest because the new law will be effective
August 12, 2026;
WHEREAS, the proposed emergency rule amendment is necessary for the
Division of Gaming to carry out the purposes of SB 26-131 and the modifications
therein by the effective date, and immediate adoption of the emergency
amendments to Rules 7.6, 7.7, and 7.11 will remove unlawful rules and allow
