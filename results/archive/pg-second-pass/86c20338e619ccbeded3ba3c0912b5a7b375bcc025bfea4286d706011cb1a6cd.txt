COMMITTEE AMENDMENT “ ” to H.P. 1395, L.D. 2080
1 SUMMARY
22 This amendment replaces the bill and changes the title. The amendment prohibits an
33 operator and a management services licensee conducting either sports wagering or Internet
44 gaming on behalf of an operator from accepting wagers from a person using a credit card.
55 It also provides that, when adopting rules for the method of operation of sports wagering
66 and Internet gaming and for the minimum design and security requirements for sports
77 wagering and Internet gaming, the director of the Gambling Control Unit within the
88 Department of Public Safety must ensure that a person does not use a credit card to make
99 a wager.
