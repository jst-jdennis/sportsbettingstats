SUMMARY
KEY TAKEAWAYS
Of 7.2 million California adults who reported gambling
in the past year, 6.7% (488,000) reported they had
symptoms of problem gambling.
Adults who reported any gambling in the past year
also reported other types of behaviors associated with
increased risk of poor health outcomes, including binge
drinking (24.4%), smoking (7.2%), use of e-cigarettes or
vapes (6.4%), and substance use (2.4%).
Nearly one-fourth (24.9%) of adults with symptoms of
problem gambling also reported experiencing serious
psychological distress in the past year, compared
with 13.5% of gambling adults without symptoms of a
gambling problem.
Summary: This fact sheet uses the 2023 Gambling Module of the
California Health Interview Survey (CHIS) data to examine gambling in
California and associated health behaviors and risks to inform future
regulatory approaches and public health interventions.
