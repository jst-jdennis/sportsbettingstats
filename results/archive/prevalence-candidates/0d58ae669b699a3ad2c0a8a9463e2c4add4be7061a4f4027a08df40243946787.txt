Gemini Research responded to a competitive bid for

gambling impact study required by CT Legislature
Contract awarded to Gemini Research and study

carried out Jan-August 2023
Examined social/health and economic/fiscal impacts

of gambling in CT since mid-1990s
Included review of state’s PG prevention and

treatment services

=== page 13 ===
Recommendations for Treatment in CT
Problem gambling prevention and treatment services in CT

provide a good model for the rest of the country
Best practices include:

Age 21 requirement for casinos, sports, online gambling
⚫
Irrevocable self-exclusion bans
⚫
Availability of lifetime bans
⚫
Automated sports betting account freezes
⚫
Areas of concern:

Total funding for services is a small fraction of $400M derived
⚫
annually from problem gamblers
Proportion of revenue drawn from people experiencing gambling
⚫
harms is too high
