Problem Gambling’s Prevalence in Oregon

Oregon Health Authority seeks to better understand the effects of gambling and problem gambling in Oregon. This includes proprietary research studies and including questions about gambling in agency surveys. 

Visit the Oregon Council on Problem Gambling website to learn more

Oregon Problem Gambling Program Reports

Use the Search and Sort functions find reports by topic or date. If you would like to view monthly Problem Gambling Helpline data, please email the Oregon Problem Gambling Program team.
