SEIGMA PROJECT OVERVIEW
SEIGMA is a project that uses experts from several disciplines to carry out the different parts of the Massachusetts Gaming
Commission's research effort. The project team is dedicated to working closely with many other groups throughout the
Commonwealth. The study uses a state-of-the-art design, rigorous data collection and research methods, and a careful analytic
approach to establish the effects of casino gambling at state, regional, and local levels. The SEIGMA team collects its own data
as well as using data gathered by other organizations. The SEIGMA study is funded by the Massachusetts Gaming Commission.
www.umass.edu/seigma
