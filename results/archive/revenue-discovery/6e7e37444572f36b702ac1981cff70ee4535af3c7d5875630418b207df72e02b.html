Monthly Reports

Sports Wagering Monthly Reports

In-Person Sports Wagering went live in Illinois on March 9, 2020.

The Monthly Sports Wagering Reports and the corresponding Comma Separated Values (CSV) File agree to Illinois Gaming Board data. The CSV file can be downloaded into other file types and managed for the ease of the user, however, the Illinois Gaming Board is not accountable for the reliability of disseminated information resulting from changes to the Illinois Gaming Board's data that are made by the user. Please note the CSV file may require additional formatting upon access.

Due to the COVID-19 crisis, sports wagering operations were suspended on March 16, 2020. Online wagering began on June 18, 2020 and in-person wagering resumed on July 1, 2020.

Note: Sports Wagering revenue figures are not due to the Illinois Gaming Board until the last day of the month following the month the revenue was earned. Earliest revenue data was reported for March 2020. 

NOTE: All PDF and CSV files will automatically download to your Downloads folder on your computer. On most browsers, the fastest way to access your recent downloads is to click on the Downloads icon located on the top right of your browser [on PCs you can also use Ctrl+J as a shortcut]. Please also note that some reports may take up to 30 seconds to download.
