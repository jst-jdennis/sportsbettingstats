Division of Gaming

Division of Gaming

The Division of Gaming oversees the following types of gaming in New York State:

  • Sports Wagering
  • Commercial Casinos
  • Video Lottery
  • Indian Gaming
  • Interactive Fantasy Sports

 

Regulated Gaming Industries

card image

Sports Wagering

Mobile sports wagers are made electronically from a location in NYS and transmitted to a licensed commercial casino.
card image

Commercial Casinos

Commercial casino facilities are traditional casinos with live dealers, table games, slot machines and poker.
card image

Video Lottery

Video lottery terminals and electronic table games are linked to a centralized system that tracks game play.
card image

Indian Gaming

The Indian Gaming Regulatory Act permits federally recognized Indian tribes to conduct gaming activities.
card image

Interactive Fantasy Sports (IFS)

Participants in fantasy sports assemble a roster of athletes and use their actual performance statistics to compete.
