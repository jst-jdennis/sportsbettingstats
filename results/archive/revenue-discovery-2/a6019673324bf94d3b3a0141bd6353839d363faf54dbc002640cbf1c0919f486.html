Michigan iGaming, Online Sports Betting Operators Report $341.3M in June Revenue

  2. News

July 21, 2026

DETROIT, July 21, 2026 — Michigan commercial and tribal operators reported a combined $341.3 million in internet gaming (iGaming) and online sports betting gross receipts in June, a 10.8% decrease from May 2026.

Monthly Gross Receipts
June iGaming gross receipts totaled $301.2 million. June online sports betting gross receipts reached $40.1 million.

Monthly Adjusted Gross Receipts
Combined adjusted gross receipts (AGR) totaled $309.36 million for June, including $289.21 million from iGaming and $20.15 million from online sports betting.

Compared with May 2026, iGaming AGR decreased 1.5% and online sports betting AGR declined 13.4%. Year-over-year, iGaming AGR was up 28.0%, while online sports betting AGR decreased by 33.9%.

Monthly Handle
Online sports betting handle totaled $366.1 million, a 20.0% decrease from May 2026.

Monthly State Taxes and Payments
Operators submitted $62.3 million in taxes and payments to the State of Michigan in June, including:

  • iGaming taxes and fees: $60.8 million
  • Online sports betting taxes and fees: $1.5 million


Monthly City of Detroit Taxes and Payments
The three Detroit casinos reported $13.96 million in wagering taxes and municipal services fees paid to the City of Detroit in June, including:

  • iGaming taxes and fees: $13.5 million
  • Online sports betting taxes and fees: $460,753


Monthly Tribal Operators’ Payments
Tribal operators reported $8.4 million in payments to governing bodies in June.

Additional Information
A revenue distribution table for online gaming and sports betting is available on the agency’s website.

As of June 2026, 15 commercial and tribal operators have been authorized to offer iGaming and/or online sports betting in Michigan. Of those, 13 operators offer online sports betting, while all 15 offer iGaming. Detailed results by operator are available on the MGCB website.

 


Gambling in any form is for entertainment purposes only. If you or someone you know may have a gambling problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER, chat online, or visit 800gambler.org. Help is available 24/7, free and confidential. Michigan residents can also visit the MGCB's Responsible Gaming page for information on self-exclusion programs, including the Disassociated Persons List and the Internet Gaming and Sports Betting Responsible Gaming Database. Through a partnership with Gamban, Michigan residents can claim a free license for Gamban's gambling blocking software — visit Michigan.gov/MGCB to learn more. Additional responsible gaming tools are available at DontRegretTheBet.org. The Michigan Gaming Control Board shall ensure the conduct of fair and honest gaming to protect the interests of Michigan citizens. Learn more at Michigan.gov/MGCB.
