DHS-DPBH - PROBLEM GAMBLING
101-3200
PROGRAM DESCRIPTION
The purpose of this program is to mitigate negative impacts of problem gambling. Established during the 2005 Legislative Session, this revolving account supports programs for the
prevention and treatment of problem gambling. The funds must be expended only to award grants of money or contracts for services to state agencies and other political subdivisions of the
state or to organizations or educational institutions to provide programs for the prevention and treatment of problem gambling or to provide services related to the development of data, the
assessment of needs, the performance of evaluations or technical assistance. Statutory Authority: NRS 458A
BASE
This request continues one position and associated operating costs. One-time expenditures have been eliminated and partial year costs have been annualized.
