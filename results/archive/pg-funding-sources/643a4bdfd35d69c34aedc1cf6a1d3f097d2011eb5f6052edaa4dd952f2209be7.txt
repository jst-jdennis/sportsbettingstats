Sports Wagering Definitions
• Handle: Amount players wagered, including promotional wagers provided by sportsbooks • Contributions to the State: The portion of operators’ taxable win that was
• Prizes: Winnings paid to players directed to the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future Fund.
• Hold %: Percentage of the handle remaining after prizes are paid • Promotional Play: Complimentary play provided to customers by sports
• Other Deductions: Includes federal excise tax and loss carryforward wagering operators.
• Taxable Win: Amount remaining after sportsbooks pay winners and deduct
promotional play and other amounts
Sports Wagering FY2025 Facts & Figures
• All mobile and retail operators: Through May 2025, contribution to the State was 15% of taxable win directed to the Blueprint
for Maryland’s Future Fund.
• Mobile operators: Contribution to the State increased to 20% beginning in June 2025.
- For June 2025 only, the entire amount was directed to the Blueprint.
- From July 2025 onward, mobile operators pay 15% to the Blueprint and 5% to the General Fund.
• Retail operators: Contribution to the State remains 15% of taxable win directed to the Blueprint.
• Expired/withheld sports wagering prizes paid to the Problem Gambling Fund in FY2025: $1,329,243.
Fantasy Competition FY2025 Facts & Figures
The operators of fantasy competitions (including daily fantasy sports) contribute 15% of their proceeds to the Blueprint for
Maryland’s Future Fund. In FY2025, total fantasy competition proceeds were $6,996,317, distributed as follows:
• Operators’ Share: $5,946,870 (85%) • Blueprint for Maryland’s Future Fund: $1,049,448 (15%)
Racing and Community Development Facilities Fund Transfers
In FY2025, Maryland Lottery and Gaming received $5 Million from the Horse Racing Purse Dedication Account, $3.5 Million from the Pimlico
Community Development Authority, and $10,847,018 from the Racetrack Facility Renewal Account, totaling $19,374,018. Per the Racing and Community
Development Act of 2020, the $19,374,018 was then allocated as follows: $17 Million to the Racing and Community Development Facilities Fund and
$2,374,018 to the General Fund.
Maryland Lottery and Gaming encourages responsible gaming. Players must be at least 18 to buy Maryland Lottery tickets or participate
in fantasy competitions, and must be at least 21 to gamble at Maryland's casinos or place sports wagers. Call 1-800-GAMBLER or visit
mdgamblinghelp.org to learn about free counseling and other resources.
