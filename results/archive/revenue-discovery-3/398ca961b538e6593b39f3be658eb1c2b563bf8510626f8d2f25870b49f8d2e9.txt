REVENUE REPORTING NOTES
1. Sports Gaming Receipts, as defined by Rev.Code §5753.01, are comprised of the following components:
Gross Receipts (+) Total Gross Receipts Rev.Code 5753.01(H)
Winnings Paid (-) All cash and cash equivalents paid as winnings to sports gaming patrons Rev.Code 5753.01(H)(1)
Voided Wagers (-) The dollar amount of all voided wagers. Rev.Code 5753.01(H)(2)
Promotional (-) *Eligible portion of promotional gaming credits (See Schedule Below) Rev.Code 5753.01(H)(4)
Revenue = Sports Gaming Receipts Rev.Code 5753.01(H)
2. Amounts in the Revenue category which are less than zero are shown as zero in the Taxable Revenue category. Losses may not be carried
forward to future periods.
3. Amounts shown are as reported to the Ohio Casino Control Commission and may differ from amounts reported to the Ohio Department
of Taxation.
4. Adjustments to the listed information may be necessary and will be made with future postings.
*Promotional represents the total amount wagered using promotional funds. These amounts are included in the Total Gross Receipts
figures. Beginning in 2027, a portion of this total may be deducted to arrive at Sports Gaming Receipts (aka Revenue), in accordance with
the following schedule, per Rev.Code 5753.01(H)(4)(a) and (b):
On and after January 1, 2027, but before January 1, 2032, ten per cent of the promotional gaming credits wagered by patrons;
On and after January 1, 2032, twenty per cent of the promotional gaming credits wagered by patrons.
